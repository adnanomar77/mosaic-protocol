Verified requirements relevant to MOSAIC:

1. Ledger publishes full-length original research on cryptocurrencies, blockchains, and DeFi, including new computer algorithms/protocols. Technical submissions should explain broader relevance beyond a single application.
2. Research articles are rarely shorter than 4,000 words and can become unwieldy over 10,000 words.
3. Submissions must be PDF files created using the Ledger template and must include a cover letter. The cover letter should describe novelty, fit, and at least three suggested reviewers with contact details.
4. Authors must disclose use of AI/LLMs. Ledger may decline work involving undeclared or improper AI use. Reference URLs should be provided when available, with DOI links preferred.
5. Authors must make materials necessary to evaluate the findings freely available, including source code and raw data where relevant.
6. The manuscript structure should include title, author/affiliation, <=200-word abstract, keywords, introduction, body sections, conclusion, appendixes as needed, acknowledgments, author contributions, conflicts of interest, and notes/references.
7. Ledger expects rigorous validation appropriate to the field. For computer-system work this can include performance benchmarks, and artifacts/data are encouraged for reproducibility.
8. The journal is open access and publishes under CC BY after acceptance. It asks the corresponding author to digitally sign the final formatted PDF; this cannot be performed before the author supplies or uses the relevant signing key/wallet.

The manuscript can omit the optional email in the author footnote, but the submission platform will still require the author to use his own account email.
10. The final package must include an explicit AI-use disclosure rather than attempting to conceal tool assistance. The author remains responsible for verification, claims, references, code, and submission.
